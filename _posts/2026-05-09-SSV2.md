It has always been a challenge to accurately measure the kinematics of human motion with wearable sensors for the purposes of exoskeleton control and machine learning. As a graduate student in the EPIC lab at Georgia Tech, I created a custom 9-axis Inertial measurement unit to perform real time measurements of human motion to replace extremely expensive, aerospace grade IMUs with little to no sacrifice in performance.

## Design Overview
![SSV2 PCB]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/SSV2/SSV2_PCB.png){: .img-wrap-left}
The custom IMU consists of 3 sensors that when combined, allow for accurate Euler angle measurements:
-__ICM 42607 Accelecrometer + Gyroscope__ provides the foundational measurements for Kalman filter sensor fusion using the absolute gravity vector.
-__MLX 90393 Magnetometer__ provides the additional North vector for the Kalman filter to accurately calculate Euler yaw.
-__BMP581 Pressure sensor__ provides a highly accurate pressure reading, which at the scale of humans, can provide relative height measurements.

In addition to these ICs, the IMU also features a proprietary I2C over USB-C interface to take advantage of the sheided twisted wires of the USB-C assembly to allow I2C signal to travel more than 1 meter at high speed + (1MHz) without dropout.

## Deployment and Comparison
![SSV2 Deployment on subject]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/SSV2/SSV2_Deployment.png){: .img-wrap-left}
Here, the SSV2 is mounted along with the Microstrain 3DM-GX5 (Ultra high end IMU costing $2000+) on a test subject along with motion capture dots to track ground truth joint kinematics. The test results are then fed into the EPIC Lab's machine learning algorithms that predict joint moments from kinematic input.

## Results
![SSV2 comparison]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/SSV2/SSV2_Comparison.png){: .img-breakout}
The comparison results in a R-squared of 0.99+, showing near identical output between the two sensors. This allows the lab to replace __$2000+__ IMUs with custom PCBs with a unit cost of __$16__ with no sacrifice in performance.
