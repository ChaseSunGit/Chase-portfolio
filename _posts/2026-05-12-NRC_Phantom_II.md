Methane is a highly potent greenhouse gas that has been historically poorly measured. There is an increasing demand for portable, sensitive, and cost-effective instruments to better understand the emission of methane and its effect on the changing climate. As a mechantronics engineer at the National Research Council (NRC) between 2021 and 2024, I developed a laser-based methane sensor that allows climate scientists to accuately measure methane emissions with an ultra light-weight, UAV deployable package, weighing only 890g and measuring with a sensitivity of sub 1 parts-per-billion.
